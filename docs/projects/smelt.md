# SMELT — NASA Big Idea Challenge 2023

**SMELT:** *Sintering & Melting Extraction via Lunar Thermal Processing*  
*(Reference: [Penn State Engineering News Article](https://www.psu.edu/news/engineering/story/nasa-funded-student-team-builds-microwave-system-smelt-metal-moon/))*

---

## 📌 Mission Overview

Selected by NASA as one of seven university teams nationwide, SSPL developed **SMELT** for the **2023 NASA BIG Idea Challenge** (Breakthrough, Innovative and Game-changing Idea).

SMELT uses a custom **microwave sintering system** to smelt lunar regolith minerals—specifically **ilmenite ($FeTiO_3$)**—to extract raw metals and oxygen on the lunar surface. By using microwave energy, the system rapidly heats and fuses regolith particles with significantly lower power consumption than traditional resistive heating methods.

---

## 💡 Key Technical Innovations

1. **Microwave Energy Sintering:** Rapid volumetric heating of ilmenite-rich simulated lunar soil without melting containment crucibles.
2. **In-situ Catalyst Integration:** Combining microwave plastic pyrolysis gases with mineral reduction to boost methane and metal yields.
3. **Compact Thermal Chamber:** Structural design optimized for payload mass limits on lunar landers.
