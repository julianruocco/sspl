## 📌 Project Overview

As NASA prepares for long-duration habitation on the Moon under the **Artemis Program**, managing waste stream products—including plastic packaging, metallic scrap, fabric, and inorganic trash—becomes vital for mission sustainability.

The **Student Space Programs Laboratory (SSPL)** was selected as a **Phase 2 Finalist** in the NASA LunaRecycle Challenge. The SSPL team is designing a compact, low-mass physical recycling architecture capable of converting lunar trash into usable manufacturing feedstocks, structural elements, and methane/syngas fuel.

---

## 🔬 Technical Architecture

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                 SSPL LUNARECYCLE RECOVERY PIPELINE               │
├─────────────────┬───────────────────┬───────────────────────────┤
│ 1. Shred & Sort │ 2. Microwave Heat │ 3. Product Extraction     │
│ Waste Shredding │ Microwave Pyrolysis│ Methane / Syngas Recovery │
│ Material Class  │ Thermal Reactor   │ Recycled Polymer Feedstock│
└─────────────────┴───────────────────┴───────────────────────────┘
```

- **Inorganic & Plastic Pyrolysis:** Utilizing targeted microwave radiation to decompose polymer waste into clean hydrocarbons and combustible syngas.
- **Closed-Loop Processing:** Minimal water/consumable usage tailored for severe lunar thermal-vacuum environments.
- **Autonomous Digital Twin Integration:** Physics-predictive digital twin framework monitoring thermal gradients and reactor efficiency in real time.
