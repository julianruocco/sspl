# National Eclipse Ballooning Project (NEBP)

The **National Eclipse Ballooning Project (NEBP)** is a NASA and NSF co-sponsored initiative uniting university teams to conduct atmospheric scientific observations during major solar eclipse events.

---

## 🎈 Payload Capabilities

SSPL engineered specialized weather balloon payloads flown to altitudes exceeding **90,000 feet (stratosphere)**:

- **Atmospheric Profiling:** Temperature, humidity, barometric pressure, and wind vector sensing.
- **Radiation Measurement:** Geiger counter sensors monitoring cosmic radiation changes during solar occlusions.
- **Live Video Telemetry:** Onboard high-definition video downlink transmitting real-time stratospheric eclipse views.
