# Flight Program & CubeSats

The **Flight Program** is SSPL's flagship hardware integration division. It encompasses all payload developments for orbital CubeSats, sounding rockets, and suborbital flight platforms.

---

## 🛰️ Featured CubeSat Mission: OSPREY 3U

**OSPREY (Ocean Surface Plastic & Radiation Environmental Yield):** A 3U CubeSat architecture designed to collect orbital data and track marine debris.

### Subsystem Breakdown:
- **Command & Data Handling (CDH):** Microcontroller architecture running real-time edge-computing algorithms for spectral image compression prior to downlink.
- **Communications (COMM):** Dual-band UHF beacon transceiver and high-speed S-Band ground station link (integrating Lehigh University and PSU ground stations).
- **Electrical Power System (EPS):** Deployable solar arrays with Lithium-ion battery charge management.
- **Structures & Thermal (STR/THM):** CNC-machined aluminum 6061-T6 chassis with active thermal regulators.

## 🛠️ Suborbital Sounding Rockets

In partnership with NASA Wallops Flight Facility, SSPL students design, build, and fly suborbital scientific payloads reaching microgravity environments.

- **Launch Vehicle:** Terrier-Improved Orion sounding rockets.
- **Testing Standard:** NASA GEVS (General Environmental Verification Standard) vibration and TVAC qualification.
