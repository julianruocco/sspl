# SSPL Past Projects Archive

Since August 2006, SSPL has successfully completed dozens of student space hardware missions.

---

## 📜 Selected Legacy Missions

1. **OSIRIS 3U CubeSat:** Solar space weather monitoring mission concept.
2. **NASA Microgravity Flight Experiments:** Parabolic flight payloads testing fluid dynamics and microgravity particle behavior.
3. **HASP (High Altitude Student Platform):** Stratospheric balloon payloads flown annually with LSU/NASA.
4. **NASA Sounding Rocket Payloads (RockSat-C / RockSat-X):** High-g sounding rocket payloads measuring upper atmosphere ionospheric plasma.
