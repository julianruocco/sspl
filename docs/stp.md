# Student Training Program (STP)

The **Student Training Program (STP)** is SSPL's signature onboarding curriculum designed to turn enthusiastic students into capable space systems engineers.

---

## 🎯 Program Overview

STP runs twice a year to coincide with academic semester intakes:
- **Fall Intake:** Initiated every **September** &rarr; Launches in **December**
- **Spring Intake:** Initiated every **January** &rarr; Launches in **May**

No prior aerospace experience is required! STP teaches hands-on skills in systems engineering, circuit design, CAD modeling, microcontrollers, and team coordination.

---

## 📚 STP Semester Curriculum & Milestones

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                      STP 14-WEEK TRAINING CYCLE                         │
├────────────────────┬────────────────────┬───────────────────────────────┤
│ Weeks 1 - 4        │ Weeks 5 - 9        │ Weeks 10 - 14                 │
│ Fundamentals & CAD │ Circuit Fabrication│ Assembly, Integration & Test  │
│ Systems Intro      │ Microcontrollers   │ High-Altitude Balloon Launch  │
└────────────────────┴────────────────────┴───────────────────────────────┘
```

### Module 1: Systems Engineering & Design Tools
- Introduction to NASA Systems Engineering handbook concepts.
- CAD drafting with SolidWorks and Onshape.
- Requirement writing and trade studies.

### Module 2: Electronics & Embedded Software
- Soldering certification and breadboard prototyping.
- Arduino / STM32 / Raspberry Pi microcontroller programming.
- Sensor integration (IMUs, temperature, pressure, GPS).

### Module 3: Payload Integration & Launch
- Structural payload assembly and mass properties balancing.
- Environmental testing (thermal, drop test, vibration).
- Flight launch on an SSPL high-altitude balloon and telemetry recovery!
