# Facilities & Equipment

Fabrication, integration, and environmental testing are critical phases of any space hardware mission. SSPL maintains dedicated engineering facilities at Penn State University Park, supplemented by access to shared college labs.

---

## 🛠️ SSPL Primary Laboratory Facilities

### 1. Electronics Prototyping & Assembly Lab
- **Location:** <span class="fill-in-highlight">[FILL IN: e.g., Hammond Building / EE East]</span>
- **Capabilities:** High-reliability ESD-safe soldering stations, digital storage oscilloscopes, logic analyzers, RF spectrum analyzers, electrical multimeters, circuit breadboards, and PCB prototyping tools.
- **Usage:** Custom PCB assembly for EPS, CDH, and sensor payload electronics.

### 2. Integration & Cleanroom Facility
- **Class:** ISO Class 8 (10,000) Clean Bench Environment
- **Capabilities:** Cleanroom garmenting, laminar flow hoods, optical inspection microscopes, clean vacuum chamber, and ESD grounding monitoring.
- **Usage:** Final payload assembly, optics cleaning, and CubeSat integration.

### 3. Mechanical & Composite Fabrication Shop
- **Capabilities:** CNC mills, precision lathes, 3D printing suite (PLA, ABS, PETG, high-temp nylon), sheet metal tools, composite layup vacuum bagging.
- **Usage:** Structural chassis machining, custom brackets, and enclosure fabrication.

### 4. Satellite Ground Station & UHF/S-Band Communications
- **Capabilities:** Roof-mounted tracking yagi antenna array, software-defined radios (SDRs), automated TLE orbit tracking, and Lehigh Ground Station network integration.
- **Usage:** Telemetry downlink, beacon tracking, and command uplink.

---

## 🔬 Partner & Penn State Shared Facilities

SSPL students also leverage Penn State's advanced research infrastructure:
- **Penn State Nanofabrication Laboratory:** Cleanroom facilities for micro-fabrication.
- **Acoustic & Vibration Testing Labs:** Random vibration and modal testing for launch qualification.
- **Thermal Vacuum Chamber (TVAC):** Space thermal and vacuum environmental simulation.
