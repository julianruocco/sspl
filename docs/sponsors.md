# Sponsors & Sponsorship Tiers

Sponsors make student space research and flight hardware possible! Contributions directly fund student travel to launch sites, raw materials, cleanroom supplies, testing equipment, and high-altitude balloon flights.

---

## 💎 Corporate Sponsorship Tiers

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                      SSPL SPONSORSHIP LEVELS                            │
├────────────────────┬────────────────────┬───────────────────────────────┤
│ Orbital ($10,000+) │ Lunar ($5,000+)    │ Stratospheric ($1,000+)       │
│ Logo on Payload    │ Logo on Apparel    │ Logo on Website & Reports     │
│ Resume Book Access │ Access to Recruits │ Lab Tour & Student Briefings  │
└────────────────────┴────────────────────┴───────────────────────────────┘
```

### 🛰️ Orbital Tier ($10,000+)
- Prominent logo placement on SSPL flight hardware payloads and CubeSats.
- Direct access to SSPL graduating senior resume book.
- Exclusive annual presentation & technical demonstration by student leads.
- Full recognition on all lab banners, website homepage, and publications.

### 🌙 Lunar Tier ($5,000+)
- Logo on team apparel, lab banners, and website sponsor wall.
- Direct recruiting info-session with SSPL student members.
- Annual project progress reports.

### 🎈 Stratospheric Tier ($1,000+)
- Logo on SSPL website sponsor section.
- Invitation to annual lab showcase.
