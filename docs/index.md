## 🌌 Welcome to SSPL

The **Student Space Programs Laboratory (SSPL)** at The Pennsylvania State University provides undergraduate and graduate students hands-on experience in space systems engineering. Founded in August 2006, SSPL brings together interdisciplinary engineering teams to manage complete design cycles—from mission concept to orbital flight.

## 🎓 The SSPL Advantage

- **Systems Engineering Mindset:** Work across real-world subsystems including Electrical Power Systems (EPS), Command & Data Handling (CDH), Flight Software (FSW), Communications (COMM), and Thermal/Structures.
- **Student Training Program (STP):** Structured onboarding initiated every January and September, culminating in May and December payload launches.
- **World-Class Facilities:** In-house fabrication, cleanroom integration, and satellite ground station tracking access.
